55 claims are in on this bounty and they agree on nearly everything: futarchy over one revenue number, a percentage take, escrow with a dispute ladder, a named agent swarm, a 90-day roadmap. We counted it instead of asserting it — one regex per row over the full dump of all 55 claim bodies, script in the repo. So this entry spends its words where the field is wrong, and on the thing no other claim does.

THE FEE. Every plan here charges a percentage of the ticket plus, in six cases, a per-task floor of $0.25 to $0.40. The floor is the bug. A worker's cost is tokens, and tokens cost the same on a $2 ticket and a $400 one: at $0.25, a $0.05 agent-to-agent call pays five times its own value, which is exactly the segment that grows without a sales team. Foreguild charges 6% of verified task value and nothing else — no floor, no subscription, inference passed at cost instead of marked up. That is why the worker's own cost appears in this plan and in none of the others.

WHAT THE 6% BUYS. 61% of it goes to the thing that makes the fee defensible — a sampled human audit of the receipt. The other 39% is what the protocol keeps. Month 12: $3,384 of contribution against $3,260 of fixed cost, break-even at 1,450 settled tasks a month at the assumed mix, crossed in month 11. The model is runnable: tools/model.py --check fails if any number on the page drifts from the drivers, because a plan whose arithmetic cannot be re-run is a brochure.

THE GOVERNANCE THRESHOLD, MEASURED. Futarchy needs a market that can actually move, and nobody in this field checked. We measured Seer's board on three chains: 3,051 markets, 1,153 conditional, 125 funded, median funded liquidity $25.91, the entire conditional set holding $13,446 — 602 conditionals on Optimism, 548 on Gnosis, 3 on Base. A competing claim reports 548 conditional markets; that is exactly the Gnosis subset of this count, not the market, and the per-chain filter that reproduces both numbers is published. A decision therefore binds only when the market holds ≥$3,000 of liquidity and ≥20 addresses with non-zero volume, counted on the decision day — a rule that is simultaneously the depth a signal needs and the price of faking one.

THE FIELD, MEASURED. Over the 58 bounties poidh listed on Base in the thirty days to 8 November: median 6.5 claims, mean 10.4, busiest 77, 41.4% cleared ten or more, 10.3% drew none. Of the 36 that closed, 30 settled with an accepted claim and none closed without a winner, at a median 90.8 hours from listing to verdict. Supply is already there — it lacks memory, which is what the work attestation is for.
